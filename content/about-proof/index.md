Proof reads and redefines the terms of the debate about journalism and its social role.

Proof checks journalism today against its own past; and tests the future for journalism.

Proof tells the truth about journalism; and stands up for the truthfulness of journalism.

Proof is required reading for journalists seeking to defend, extend and improve their journalism.

The editor of _Proof_ is Andrew Calcutt. [Click here to get in touch.](https://proof-reading.org/contact-proof/ "Contact Proof")

The webside design and admin is by Mark Beachill. Proof's logo was created by Alex Cameron.
